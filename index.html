    How a floor plays

    Roll. Loot. Ritual. Break.

    1. RollFive turns on the board. Every pip is a step.
    2. LootRunes, gold, chests, merchants and shrines.
    3. RitualSlot your spells and runes. Plan the chain.
    4. BreakThe ritual plays out. The Guardian falls, or you do.

    What you do

    Features

    Expedition board

    Six planes, 25 floors each. Roll the dice, walk the board, loot chests, find runes, meet merchants and dodge curses before the Guardian wakes.

    Ritual battles

    Preparation wins the fight. Fill the ritual slots, chain runes, stack fire, ice, lightning, chaos and physical ailments, then watch the cast break the Guardian.

    Forbidden Arts and Signatures

    Each job has a Signature skill and one Forbidden Art: a finisher you recover scroll by scroll and unleash when its gauge is full.

    The Endless Realm

    A whole island under fog. Towns, ruins, watchtowers and boss lairs; Bonded Beasts, a Nemesis that hunts you, fishing, mining and ten Mythic sets to forge.

    Moonhaven

    The city hub. Gear Shop, Scroll Merchant, the Constellation, the Training Camp and a story told by the people who still keep the lantern lit.

    Ritual Duel and more

    Duel rival Wardens with your own spells as cards, climb the Spire of Echoes, take daily missions and chase 70 achievements.
