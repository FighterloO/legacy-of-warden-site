Short description

Plan the ritual, break the Guardian. A dark fantasy roguelite of spells and runes.

About the game

The last lantern of Moonhaven still burns, and you are its Warden. Explore six planes floor by floor with the dice, gather runes and spells, then set your ritual and watch it break the Guardian. Grow your Warden through five jobs with Signature skills and Forbidden Arts, then roam the Endless Realm: an island of towns, beasts, rival Wardens, Mythic sets and the Abyss.

Key features

  • Dice-board expeditions across six planes of 25 floors
  • Ritual battles: preparation wins, combos chain five elements
  • Five jobs, each with a Signature skill and a Forbidden Art
  • The Endless Realm: towns, Bonded Beasts, Nemesis, Ritual Duel, fishing, ten Mythic sets
  • A story told by the people of Moonhaven, 70 achievements
