Three forgotten orders

The orders of the red isle

While the world forgot them, three orders kept their oaths in exile. Each carries its own gauge, a ten-node tree that forks into two roads, a Signature skill and a Forbidden Art, and each is yours the moment the pack is.

Hemomancer

Fire · Blood gauge

Every spell is paid for in Ward, and every price feeds the Blood. The fuller the vessel, the hotter the flame, until a single crimson burst drains it dry.

SignatureCrimson CovenantAll your Blood poured into 2 fire hits that always ignite, 12% stronger for every drop.
Forbidden ArtCrimson EclipseThe world bleeds red. Five slashes from the shadows, then the jaws close.

Black Knight

Physical, lightning · Iron gauge

Every spear the Black Knight drives stays in the Guardian, and every spear calls the storm. The more iron left standing, the harder the lightning falls.

SignatureIron VerdictLightning splits the sky, and every Impale is driven home as one.
Forbidden ArtIron RequiemA forest of black spears rises. Then judgement falls as thunder.

Assassin

Ice · Intent gauge

The Assassin strikes seldom, and never in vain. Each hit sharpens the Intent, and once it is full, the next blow is a certain Killing Blow.

SignatureDeath MarkAll your Intent loosed as certain ice crits. A Guardian already faltering is executed.
Forbidden ArtMoonless KillThe field freezes over. Three frost strikes from the dark, then the kill.
