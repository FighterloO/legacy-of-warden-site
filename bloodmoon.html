Three new jobs

The orders of the red isle

Each job has its own gauge, a ten-node tree with two roads, a Signature skill and a Forbidden Art. They open as soon as the pack is yours.

Hemomancer

Fire · Blood gauge

Every skill pays a little Ward and gains Blood. Blood makes your fire burn hotter until one crimson burst spends it all.

SignatureCrimson CovenantAll your Blood in 2 fire hits that always ignite, 12% more per Blood.
Forbidden ArtCrimson EclipseThe world turns red, five slashes from the shadows, then a giant bite.

Black Knight

Physical, lightning · Iron gauge

Impales stay in the Guardian, and each one is a lightning rod. Your lightning hits harder for every spear you leave behind.

SignatureIron VerdictA lightning strike, then every Impale is driven home at once.
Forbidden ArtIron RequiemA forest of black spears, then the verdict falls as lightning.

Assassin

Ice · Intent gauge

Few hits, each a decisive crit. Every hit builds Intent, and a full Intent makes your next hit a sure Killing Blow.

SignatureDeath MarkAll your Intent in sure ice crits. A weak Guardian is executed.
Forbidden ArtMoonless KillThe field freezes: three frost strikes from the dark, then the kill.

The red island
