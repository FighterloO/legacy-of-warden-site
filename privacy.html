Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Legacy of Warden ("the game") is made by Baihaqi Studio ("we"). This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and the choices you have. It covers the game on Android and on PC through Google Play Games on PC, and this website.

The short version

  • We run no servers of our own and we do not sell, rent or share your information.
  • The game has no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking.
  • Your progress is saved on your device. If you use Google Play Games, a copy is kept in your own Google account so you can continue on another device.
  • Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress in its own storage on your device: your Warden's name, levels, items, story progress, achievements and settings. It stays on your device unless you use the cloud save described below. You can delete it by deleting a Warden on the profile screen, clearing the app's data in your device settings, or uninstalling the game.

Google Play Games Services

When you start the game it may sign you in to Google Play Games with your Google account. This is optional: if you are not signed in, the game still plays fully offline. When you are signed in, Google provides these services:

  • Cloud save (Saved Games). A copy of your save profiles is stored in your Google account so you can move between phone and PC. It holds the same game progress listed above, nothing else.
  • Achievements and leaderboards. Your achievements and scores are recorded with your Play Games profile. Leaderboard entries show your Play Games name and avatar to other players, according to your Play Games visibility settings.

The game receives your Play Games player ID and display name to make these features work. We do not receive your email address or contacts. Google's handling of this data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. You can sign out, manage your Play Games profile, or delete your Play Games data in the Google Play Games app and in your Google account settings.

Purchases

The Bloodmoon Realm pack is sold through Google Play Billing. Google processes the payment; we receive only a confirmation that the purchase was made (an order reference and the product bought) so the game can unlock the pack and restore it after a reinstall. We never receive your card or payment details.

Crash reports and diagnostics

If the game crashes or stops responding, Google Play may send an anonymous technical report (device model, Android version, the error) to us through the Google Play Console, depending on your device's diagnostics settings. These reports do not identify you and are used only to fix problems.

Exporting a Warden

You can export a Warden as a file or a code to keep a copy. It is made only when you choose Export and goes only where you send it through your device's share menu. The game never uploads it.

Device backup

If your Android device backs up app data to your Google account, your saves may be included in that backup. Google runs that backup under your account and settings; we cannot see it.

Permissions

The game uses internet access only for the Google Play services above (sign-in, cloud save, achievements, leaderboards and purchases). It does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or other files.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and has no analytics. GitHub may log technical data such as IP addresses to run and protect the service; see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Some videos are hosted on YouTube. Until you press play, the site shows only its own still image and contacts YouTube not at all; when you press play, the video loads from YouTube in its privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com), and the Google Privacy Policy applies to that video.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Play Games features follow Google's rules for the account in use, including accounts managed through Google Family Link.

Changes to this policy

If a future version uses information in a new way, we will update this page before that version is released and change the date at the top.
